# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

OrbitApply is an open, welcoming community. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of age, background, experience level, nationality, or identity.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Helping others with setup and troubleshooting
- Sharing job search wins and lessons learned
- Giving constructive feedback on PRs and issues
- Being patient with new users and contributors

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sharing others' personal data without consent
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project
- Using OrbitApply to spam employers or overwhelm ATS systems

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainer directly via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shuv).

Maintainers will review all reports and take appropriate action including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all OrbitApply spaces — GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, Discord, and any other community channel.

---

*OrbitApply is built by and for job seekers. Treat others the way you'd want to be treated during a job search.*
